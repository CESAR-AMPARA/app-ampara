# Product Guide: A.M.P.A.R.A.

## Initial Concept
A.M.P.A.R.A. (Apoio, Monitoramento Psicológico e Acolhimento Responsável ao Aluno) is a web platform designed to support, monitor, and ensure psychosocial care for high school students by connecting teachers, multidisciplinary teams, and school management.

## Product Vision & Goals
- **Primary Goal:** Focus on preventive care to identify psychological risks early, while providing integrated management that centralizes communication between school staff and health professionals.

## Key Features
- **Current Capabilities:** Secure credentials-based role login with dynamic RBAC routing (locking Teachers to behavioral panels and hiding switchers), multistep onboarding (wizard), an administrative dashboard for user approval, manual teacher registration additions, interactive Class and Student registration screen prototypes (including manual form entries and bulk spreadsheet imports), a comprehensive Teacher-specific Behavioral Management Panel (featuring class selection sidebars, student emotion indicators, and a textual/voice observation recording modal with dynamic timelines), and an enhanced Coordination & Management Dashboard (supporting sub-tab navigation, real-time Class & Student instant queries, and centralized Coordination Occurrences logs linked directly to individual student behavioral histories).
- **Upcoming Features:** Backend and database integration for the registration and management of schools, classes, and students, and persistent database storage for student behavioral and coordination logs.

## Privacy & Security
- **LGPD Compliance:** Strict anonymization is enforced. Only authorized multidisciplinary teams have access to personally identifiable information (PII) and real names, ensuring that sensitive health and behavioral data is protected from unauthorized staff.

## Analytics & Reporting
- **Dashboard Focus:** The platform provides actionable insights through school-wide trends on student well-being and visualizes risk distribution to help administrators allocate resources effectively.
